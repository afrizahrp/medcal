===============================================================
1. BUSINESS RULE
===============================================================

`Device.id` is the internal MedCal system identifier.

It is generated by the system and is NOT entered by the technician.

When the upstream business flow already identifies a Master Device, the
resulting CalibrationJob MUST retain that Device relationship:

    Master Device.id
          ↓
    CalibrationJob.deviceId

Example:

Master Device:

    Device.id        = <system-generated ID>
    serialNumber     = 1234567
    customerId       = <customer>

When the CalibrationJob is created for that Device:

    CalibrationJob.deviceId = Device.id


IMPORTANT:

Do NOT derive `CalibrationJob.deviceId` from:

    technicianObservedSerial

Do NOT derive it from:

    customerDeclaredDeviceName

Do NOT create a new Device from technician input.

Do NOT use the technician's observed Serial No as a replacement for the FK.

The FK must reference the existing Master Device record.
